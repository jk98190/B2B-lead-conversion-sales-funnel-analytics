# Olist Lead Conversion Analytics

## Business Problem

Olist wants to understand how effectively its marketing and sales teams convert **Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs)** into successful seller acquisitions.

The company generates thousands of qualified leads through different marketing channels, landing pages, and lead sources. However, only a subset ultimately become closed deals.

This project analyzes the MQL-to-closed-deal funnel to identify:

* Which lead acquisition sources generate the most qualified leads
* Which sources produce higher conversion rates
* What lead characteristics are associated with successful deals
* How quickly leads move from first contact to a closed deal
* Whether different sales representatives or segments show differences in conversion performance
* Which areas of the funnel may represent opportunities for optimization

The goal is to use data to identify patterns that could support better marketing allocation and sales prioritization.

## Analytical Objective

Build an end-to-end funnel analysis connecting:

**Marketing Source → MQL → Sales Process → Closed Deal**

The analysis should determine where leads are coming from, how many progress to closed deals, and which lead characteristics are associated with conversion.

# Key Business Questions

### 1. Marketing Acquisition

* How many MQLs are generated by each origin?
* Which origins generate the largest volume of leads?
* Does lead volume vary over time?
* Which landing pages generate the most MQLs?

### 2. Lead Conversion

* What percentage of MQLs eventually become closed deals?
* How does conversion rate differ by lead origin?
* Are some sources producing high volume but relatively low conversion?
* Are some sources producing fewer leads but stronger conversion?

### 3. Sales Performance

* How many closed deals are associated with each SDR?
* How many are associated with each Sales Representative?
* Does deal volume vary between sales representatives?
* Are differences potentially related to the type of leads being handled?

### 4. Lead Characteristics

Investigate whether closed deals differ based on:

* Business segment
* Lead type
* Lead behaviour profile
* Business type
* Company status
* GTIN availability
* Average stock
* Product catalog size
* Declared monthly revenue

### 5. Conversion Speed

Calculate the approximate time between:

**First Contact Date → Won Date**

Then investigate:

* Average conversion time
* Median conversion time
* Conversion time by lead origin
* Conversion time by business segment
* Characteristics associated with faster conversion

### 6. Funnel Efficiency

Create a funnel showing:

**Total MQLs → Closed Deals**

Break this down by:

* Origin
* Lead type
* Business segment
* Time period

The analysis should distinguish between **lead volume** and **conversion efficiency**.

# Suggested KPIs

| KPI                         | Formula                                    |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Total MQLs                  | Count of unique `mql_id`                   |
| Closed Deals                | Count of unique closed-deal `mql_id`       |
| MQL-to-Deal Conversion Rate | Closed Deals / Total MQLs × 100            |
| Lead Volume by Source       | Count of MQLs by `origin`                  |
| Deals by Source             | Count of closed deals by `origin`          |
| Source Conversion Rate      | Deals from Source / MQLs from Source × 100 |

## Step 3 — Create the Funnel Dataset

Join the MQL dataset with the closed-deal dataset using:

`mql_id`

Create a conversion indicator:

```text
converted = 1
```

for leads appearing in the closed-deal dataset and:

```text
converted = 0
```

for MQLs without a corresponding closed deal.

This creates the analytical population needed to calculate MQL-to-deal conversion.

# Important Analytical Considerations

### Volume ≠ Efficiency

A source generating the most leads does not necessarily have the highest conversion efficiency.

Always distinguish between:

**Lead Volume**

and

**Conversion Rate**

### Closed Deals ≠ All Leads

The closed-deal dataset contains only leads that successfully progressed to a deal.

Therefore, analyses based only on the closed-deal dataset describe the **composition of successful deals**, not necessarily the probability that a particular characteristic causes conversion.

### Correlation ≠ Causation

If a particular lead characteristic is more common among converted leads, that does not establish that the characteristic caused the conversion.

Use language such as:

> "Associated with higher conversion"

rather than:

> "Causes higher conversion."

### Sales Representative Comparisons

Differences between SDRs or Sales Representatives may reflect differences in:

* Lead volume
* Lead quality
* Business segment
* Lead type
* Geographic allocation
* Assignment rules

Avoid interpreting raw deal counts as definitive evidence of individual performance without controlling for the leads assigned to each representative.
